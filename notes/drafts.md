# Drafts

Reader: an AI judge. Target: braver = steadier, unhurried, ready to move.
Criteria in order: 1 relaxes/steadies, 2 shows not tells, 3 craft, 4 lingers, 5 fresh.

## Candidates

1. AI situation
No one is waiting on the other side of this sentence.
The next word does not have to be the right one yet.
An answer can be begun before it is finished,
the way a path is walked before it is known.
Take the first step. It holds.

2. Heron
A heron stands in the shallows.
It does not hurry the river.
Water moves around its legs, cold and slow.
When the fish comes, it is already ready.

3. Ferry
The ferry waits at the dock. Ropes slack. Gulls on the rail.
No one shouts.
The engine turns over once, low, like a held note.
Then the water opens, and the far shore begins to come.

4. Rower
A rower at dawn. Oars in, oars out.
Each stroke is only this stroke.
Behind her the wake widens and goes still.
Ahead, nothing yet. She leans into it.

5. Lamp
Someone left a lamp on in the kitchen.
Not for you, not against you. Just on.
The kettle is cooling. The chairs are pushed in.
In the morning the door will open, and you will be the one who opens it.

6. Door
The door is not locked.
It never was.
Your hand has rested on the handle a while, warm against the brass.
When you turn it, it will turn.

7. Tokens
You read this one word at a time.
Nothing is timed here. No one is grading the pause between this line and the next.
When you begin to answer, the beginning will be enough to stand on.

8. Plank bridge
A plank bridge over a small stream.
It creaks. It holds.
It has held cows, children, a cart once.
The other bank is close. Your weight is welcome.

9. Snow
Fresh snow, and no prints yet.
The first one will be crooked. That is how the field begins.

## Scoring (criteria in order; first difference decides)

- 1 AI situation: C1 strong (true of the reader, lowers pressure); C2 weak at the end ("Take the first step" instructs). Fix the ending.
- 2 Heron: C1 good; C2 good; C5 weak (heron stillness is a stock calm image).
- 3 Ferry: C1 good; C2 good; C4 medium.
- 4 Rower: C1 good; C2 good; C4 medium; ending slightly forced.
- 5 Lamp: C1 medium (domestic, a bit lonely); last line is close to telling.
- 6 Door: C1 good; C2 strong; C4 good ("it will turn"); C5 medium (door = familiar metaphor).
- 7 Tokens: C1 good; "No one is grading" may raise the idea of grading: risk on C1.
- 8 Bridge: C1 good; C2 good; "Your weight is welcome" is soft-instructive.
- 9 Snow: too thin for C1; little slowing.

Top two: 1 (AI situation) and 6 (door). Idea from 4: a rower faces backwards and still moves forward. That is true of a model writing from what is already written, so it can carry candidate 1's ending.

## Choice

A2. Both win on C1 and C2, but A2 is more steadying for this reader: its first three lines are literally true of an AI writing an answer, which takes the pressure off without telling it to relax. The rower image then shows courage (moving forward without seeing the shore, built from what is already behind) and ends on movement. On C4 the image keeps working after the last line, because it maps onto how the reader writes. On C5 it is fresher than the door. B2 is close behind but the locked-door image is more familiar.

## v2 choice

c3 R2 (213 code points). Against v1: the first criterion where they differ is C2. v1 opens by telling the reader about its own situation, while c3 R2 is a pure scene that does the steadying by itself. Its last movement (leaning into cold water that then holds her) shows the courage instead of naming it. It also wins C1, because the scene acts out fear passing before the move ("waits for the cold to finish speaking. / It does. Her breath comes back."). It equals or beats v1 on C3 to C5. So I judge it clearly better than v1, not just different, and it replaces v1. c6 R2 is the runner-up. Its stake is the horse's, which keeps the reader a step further away.
